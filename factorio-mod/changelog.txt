Version: 0.16.144
Date: 2026-11-06
  Changes:
    - fac_belt_connect_start takes the option word "pipe" (on its own). The same router
      and queue then lay a fluid pipeline: plain steps are pipes, hops are pipe-to-ground
      pairs up to 10 tiles apart (prototype max_underground_distance 10). The entrance
      faces back toward the source, the exit toward the sink. The status command is the
      same; a missing item is reported as "Insufficient pipe items" with need_belt =
      pipes and need_underground = pipe-to-ground.
    - Pipe mode hops without an obstacle ahead. A surface pipe costs 1.5 per tile and a
      pair k + 1, so most of the run is under ground and the ground stays walkable. The
      heuristic is scaled by 1.1 (the cheapest tile, one of a longest hop). It stays
      admissible and keeps the search band of a long pipeline narrow.
    - Fluid isolation: no surface pipe stands next to an entity that can hold a fluid
      box (pipes, tanks, boilers, engines, pumps, drills, assemblers, furnaces, ...),
      except the start and goal tiles, which are meant to touch the source and the sink.
      No hop is made where a pipe-to-ground of the same axis stands within 10 tiles.
    - An end tile touching two fluid entities is refused as "start-fluid-mixed" /
      "dest-fluid-mixed", so the end pipe would not join a foreign fluid. An end with no
      usable neighbour fails fast as "start-enclosed" / "dest-enclosed". The pipe-mode
      node budget is 64000.
    - A pipe collides with characters; a belt and a pipe-to-ground do not
      (collision-mask-defaults.lua: building() vs building_walkable()). A companion
      standing on a plain pipe's tile first walks 3 tiles sideways, within 300 ticks or
      the route fails. The walker stops within 2 tiles of its target, so an aim 2 away
      would leave her on the tile. Another character on a route tile still fails the
      route as "Cannot place pipe".
    - Live (factorio-ai scripts/live_verify_pipe_route_2026_11_06.py, all PASS):
      - tank to tank, 40 tiles, across a chest wall and a foreign water line: 5 pipes + 8
        pipe-to-ground, 9747 crude in 1200 ticks, the water line stayed water only;
      - the mixed and enclosed starts are refused;
      - a route connects with the companion on its start tile, 0.3 off the centre
        either way, and on its entrance tile. Before the step-off: "Cannot place pipe".
